Common questions

Where are my goals stored? Only on your device. Acely has no account and no server.

How does app blocking work? In Settings, pick the apps to block and allow Screen Time access when iOS asks. The apps stay blocked until every goal for the day is checked off. Unchecking a goal, or the daily reset, blocks them again.

How do I turn blocking off? Use Pause or Turn off in Acely's Settings. You can also remove Acely's Screen Time access in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Can I get my goals back after deleting the app? Only from a backup you made in Acely, because nothing is stored online.
