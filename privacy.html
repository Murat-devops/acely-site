Privacy Policy

Acely: Ace Your Day · Effective November 9, 2026

The short version

Acely does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your goals, schedules, history, settings, chosen skin and backups are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Acely has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data anywhere.

Screen Time and app blocking

If you turn on app blocking, Acely uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). The apps you pick are handled by iOS as private tokens; Acely cannot see which apps they are or how you use them, and this information never leaves your device. You can revoke Screen Time access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The home-screen widget reads your goals from the device and nothing else.

Children

Acely does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
